About Logistiqoroyal

We're on a mission to make global logistics transparent, simple, and accessible for everyone.

Our Mission

Logistiqoroyal was built to solve a simple but frustrating problem: not knowing where your package is. We believe every person and business deserves real-time visibility into their shipments — without complicated software or expensive subscriptions.

Using open-source mapping technology (OpenStreetMap + Leaflet.js), we provide live, interactive tracking that works anywhere in the world, completely free of proprietary map APIs.
